Welcome to Songcraft

Songwriting brings words, melody, rhythm, and emotion together. A song might begin with a single line, a chord progression, a memory, or a sound that refuses to leave your head.

This site introduces a few practical ways to develop an idea into a complete song. Explore the pages using the navigation above.

A place to begin

Three building blocks

  1. Find an idea worth exploring.
  2. Shape the idea with images, melody, and structure.
  3. Revise until each part serves the song.
