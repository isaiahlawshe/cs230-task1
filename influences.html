Listen actively

Studying music is more useful when listening has a purpose. Instead of only asking whether a song is enjoyable, notice how it creates its effect.

  • Lyrics: What concrete images make the story feel real?
  • Melody: Which phrase is easiest to remember, and why?
  • Production: How do instruments and silence shape the mood?
  • Structure: How does the song build anticipation or release?

Borrow techniques, not identities

Influence is a starting point, not a substitute for an original voice. A writer can learn from a favorite artist's vivid details or use of contrast while writing about different experiences and choosing a personal sound.

Keep a listening journal with the song title, one technique you noticed, and one experiment you want to try in your own work.
