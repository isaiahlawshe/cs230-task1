Start with a small idea

A song does not need to begin with a complete story. A title, a contradiction, a short melody, or a striking detail can provide enough material for a first draft.

Move from draft to structure

Many popular songs use sections that create contrast and return. The table below shows one possible structure, rather than a rule every song must follow.

Example song structure
Section Purpose Possible focus
Verse Develops the story Details and context
Chorus Expresses the central idea A memorable hook
Bridge Creates a new perspective Change or revelation

Revise with intention

  • Replace vague statements with specific details.
  • Read lyrics aloud to test their natural rhythm.
  • Remove lines that repeat an idea without adding meaning.
